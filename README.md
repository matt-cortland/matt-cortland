## Indices

**[The Guinndex](https://guinndex.ai)** (Ireland)
A national price index for a pint of Guinness, built by an AI voice agent that phoned pubs to collect
prices that exist nowhere in structured data. 3,000+ pubs called across all 32 counties on St
Patrick's weekend, for roughly 200 euro in total call cost, paired with Ireland's CSO historical stout
series (which stopped in 2011).

**[The Guinndex UK](https://guinndex.co.uk)**
The same method extended to 45,000+ British pubs.

**[World Cup Pub Finder](https://guinndex.co.uk/worldcup)**
Spun out of the UK calling campaign: find the nearest pub showing your match.

**[The Gas Index](https://gasindex.ai)** (United States)
Real-time fuel prices across 170,000+ stations in all 50 states, blending EIA data back to 1990,
32,000+ AI phone calls to stations with stale prices, and crowdsourced pump-photo vision extraction.
